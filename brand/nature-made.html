Verified brands

Nature Made

Nature Made is a mass-market vitamin brand sold in US pharmacies and grocery stores. Many of its US formulas are on the public USP Verified directory. The Informed-Choice register for October 2026 lists a separate set of Nature Made names, including Super D and Super Fish Oil. Those names are not the same entries as the USP softgels and tablets. A seal on one formula does not cover the rest of the line.

Checked 15 October 2026. 71 products below. Programs: USP Verified, Informed Choice. A seal applies to the named product, not to every bottle this brand sells. Lists change; use the certifier link before you treat a seal as current.

Which certifications, and where to recheck them

  • USP Verified — These names were on the USP Verified product directory at quality-supplements.org on 15 October 2026.
  • Informed Choice — These names are on the October 2026 Informed-Choice banned-substance list published by LGC.
