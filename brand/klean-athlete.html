Klean Athlete

Last reviewed 15 October 2026 by the SupplementScore team. No named reviewer. How we choose brands.

Klean Athlete is a sport line built around banned-substance testing. The NSF Certified for Sport search returned creatine, protein, omega-3, vitamin D, iron, zinc, magnesium, collagen, and a multivitamin, among other formulas.

Checked 15 October 2026. 52 products below. Programs: NSF Certified for Sport. A seal applies to the named product, not to every bottle this brand sells. Lists change; use the certifier link before you treat a seal as current.
