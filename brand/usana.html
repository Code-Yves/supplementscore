USANA

Last reviewed 15 October 2026 by the SupplementScore team. No named reviewer. How we choose brands.

USANA sells multivitamins and omega-3s through a direct-sales model. The NSF Certified for Sport search listed CellSentials, BiOmega, MagneCal D, a prenatal pair, and SportsPak. The Informed-Choice list names a smaller set, including Vitamin D and Core Minerals. The two programs are not the same products.

Checked 15 October 2026. 18 products below. Programs: NSF Certified for Sport, Informed Choice. A seal applies to the named product, not to every bottle this brand sells. Lists change; use the certifier link before you treat a seal as current.
