NOW

Last reviewed 15 October 2026 by the SupplementScore team. No named reviewer. How we choose brands.

NOW Foods is a large supplement brand. Its vitamin line was not on the USP Verified directory when checked, so older USP claims for NOW vitamin D, magnesium, and similar products have been removed. The October 2026 Informed-Sport list does name a block of NOW Sports products. Only lines that begin with NOW Sports are included, so an unlabeled neighboring line is not treated as NOW.

Checked 15 October 2026. 35 products below. Programs: Informed Sport. A seal applies to the named product, not to every bottle this brand sells. Lists change; use the certifier link before you treat a seal as current.
