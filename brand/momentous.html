Verified brands

Momentous

Last reviewed 15 October 2026 by the SupplementScore team. No named reviewer. How we choose brands.

Momentous sells single-ingredient sport and health products. Almost everything below is NSF Certified for Sport. One product, Momentous Tongkat Ali, is on the October 2026 Informed-Sport list. The rest of the line is not described as Informed-Sport here.

Checked 15 October 2026. 78 products below. Programs: NSF Certified for Sport, Informed Sport. A seal applies to the named product, not to every bottle this brand sells. Lists change; use the certifier link before you treat a seal as current.

Each link is the public certifier or licence page checked on 15 October 2026. A retailer page, an affiliate link, or the brand's own advertisement is not a source for these listings.
