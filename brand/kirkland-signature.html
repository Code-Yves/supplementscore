Kirkland Signature

Last reviewed 15 October 2026 by the SupplementScore team. No named reviewer. How we choose brands.

Kirkland Signature is Costco's store brand. The USP Verified directory lists vitamins, minerals, fish oil, krill oil, and joint tablets under this name. One whey flavor is on the Informed-Choice list. Many other Kirkland supplements are not on either list.

Checked 15 October 2026. 17 products below. Programs: USP Verified, Informed Choice. A seal applies to the named product, not to every bottle this brand sells. Lists change; use the certifier link before you treat a seal as current.
