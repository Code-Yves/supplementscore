Nutrafol

Last reviewed 15 October 2026 by the SupplementScore team. No named reviewer. How we choose brands.

Nutrafol sells hair-growth nutraceuticals. The NSF Certified for Sport search listed the women's, men's, vegan, postpartum, and balance formulas, plus several add-on nutraceuticals. Certification here is the sport screen, not evidence that the formula grows hair.

Checked 15 October 2026. 19 products below. Programs: NSF Certified for Sport. A seal applies to the named product, not to every bottle this brand sells. Lists change; use the certifier link before you treat a seal as current.
