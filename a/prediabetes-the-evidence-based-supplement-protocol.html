Prediabetes: The Evidence-Based Supplement Protocol

6 min read ·

Prediabetes — HbA1c 5.7–6.4% or impaired fasting glucose / impaired glucose tolerance — is the window where intervention prevents progression to type 2 diabetes. The Diabetes Prevention Program established lifestyle change (5–7% weight loss + 150 minutes/week activity) is more effective than metformin. Supplements sit underneath the lifestyle layer with modest but real glycemic effects.

Berberine, 500 mg Three Times Daily

Berberine 1,500 mg/day lowers fasting glucose by ~0.5 mmol/L and HbA1c by ~0.7 percentage points in pooled meta-analytic data. Effect comparable to metformin in head-to-head trials. Watch CYP3A4 interactions. Take with the largest meals. See berberine piece.

Soluble Fiber — Psyllium 10–15 g Daily or Oat Beta-Glucan 3 g Daily

Psyllium 10.2 g daily lowers fasting glucose by 11 mg/dL and HbA1c by ~0.4 percentage points in pooled Cochrane data for impaired glucose tolerance. beta-glucan.html">Oat beta-glucan has FDA cholesterol claim and similar postprandial glucose effects. See psyllium piece.

Alpha-Lipoic Acid (ALA), 600 mg Daily

ALA produces modest improvements in fasting insulin and HOMA-IR in prediabetic adults. The 2018 meta-analysis of 18 RCTs found small but significant reductions in fasting glucose. Beware of rare insulin autoimmune syndrome (Hirata's disease) in East Asians.

Chromium Picolinate — Conditional, 200 mcg Daily

Chromium has modest glycemic effects largely confined to adults with marginal dietary intake. The Cochrane review concluded chromium did not significantly improve glycemic control in most populations. Worth 200 mcg daily if dietary intake is poor; not worth bottle space otherwise.

Magnesium, 300 mg Elemental Daily

Magnesium intake is inversely associated with progression to diabetes. Repletion in deficient adults improves insulin sensitivity. Glycinate or citrate forms.

What NOT to Take

Cinnamon's glycemic effect is small and inconsistent — see our cinnamon piece. Bitter melon and gymnema have positive pilot data but trial quality is poor. Apple cider vinegar pills do nothing despite the marketing — see ACV piece. Skip "diabetes detox" formulas entirely. Don't substitute supplements for the lifestyle layer.

How to Run the Protocol

Get baseline HbA1c, fasting glucose, fasting insulin (calculate HOMA-IR). Lifestyle layer first — 5–7% weight loss + 150 minutes/week activity has the largest effect. Add psyllium 10 g daily early — clean safety profile. Add berberine 500 mg TID at week 4 if postprandial glucose monitoring shows excursions above 180 mg/dL. Add ALA at week 8 if HOMA-IR still elevated. Re-test HbA1c at month 4–5; a drop of 0.3+ percentage points is meaningful. See the pre-diabetes stack.
