Bottom line

These are not ranked by how well they raise a blood level. 15 brands had a vitamin D product on a public list on 15 October 2026. The adult upper limit is 4,000 IU a day, so a 5,000 IU softgel is above that limit unless a clinician is treating a deficiency. Jamieson's listed vitamin D licence is a 400 IU children's chewable. An NPN is a licence to sell in Canada, not a purity seal. We do not use affiliate links.

Dose first, seal second. The allowance and the winter context are in vitamin D in a Canadian winter. Vitamin D3 is the form on most of these labels. This table only includes names we saw on a certifier or licence page.

What the programs mean

USP Verified is a specific formula on the USP list. NSF Certified for Sport and Informed-Sport are aimed at drug-tested athletes. Informed-Choice is a separate consumer list. A Health Canada NPN is permission to sell that natural health product. Nature Made, Kirkland Signature, and Member's Mark vitamin D softgels in the table are USP examples where the directory named them. Nordic Naturals sport vitamin D gummies are NSF examples. They are not automatically better than a USP 1,000 IU softgel for an adult who only needs the dietary allowance.
