Bottom line

A certification is not a sleep study. 10 brands had a magnesium product on a public list on 15 October 2026. Where the list included a glycinate, bisglycinate, or citrate, that name is the example. Combination calcium tablets are included only when magnesium is in the product name that appeared on the list. The seal covers that product, not every magnesium the brand sells.

Which salt to take is magnesium glycinate vs citrate and best magnesium for sleep. This page does not repeat that choice. It lists products whose names we saw on a certifier page. Magnesium glycinate and magnesium citrate are the ingredient pages.
