Bottom line

18 brands had an omega-3 or fish-oil product on a public list we could open on 15 October 2026. The table is one example per brand. It is not an IFOS ranking, because IFOS scores were not used. Nordic Naturals entries are the Sport SKUs that appeared in the NSF search, not a claim about every Ultimate Omega bottle. Jamieson Omega 3 Select is a Canadian licence for a defined EPA and DHA amount, not a purity seal.

Add EPA and DHA before you compare bottles. How those numbers relate to trials is in how much omega-3 per day. Omega-3 (EPA/DHA) is the ingredient page. Nothing here is a paid placement.

Seals that are easy to mix up

USP Verified fish oils, including several Nature Made and Kirkland Signature softgels, were on the USP directory. NSF Sport and Informed-Sport products were on those athletic lists. A product on one list is not on the others by default. We did not mark any product "IFOS 5-star" or "ConsumerLab approved." Those results were not on a public page we rechecked.
