How much omega-3 per day

By the SupplementScore team · Updated 15 October 2026 · No ads or affiliate links
Bottom line

For general health, food-based guidance clusters around a few hundred milligrams of EPA plus DHA a day, not several grams. The 4 gram icosapent ethyl dose in REDUCE-IT was a prescription regimen in a selected high-risk group, and a different high-dose product failed in the STRENGTH trial. Higher omega-3 intakes have also been tied to a higher chance of atrial fibrillation. Read the EPA and DHA lines on the label. The milligrams of fish oil are not the same number.

A fish-oil softgel that says 1,000 mg is usually the weight of the oil, not the weight of EPA plus DHA. Omega-3 (EPA/DHA) dosing starts with those two numbers added together.

Everyday intake versus a treatment dose

European dietary-reference work set an adequate intake of 250 mg of EPA plus DHA a day for healthy adults (EFSA Journal 2010). That is a diet target, not a treatment. A 2017 American Heart Association science advisory reviewed fish-oil supplements for preventing clinical cardiovascular disease (PMID 28289069). It is not a blank check for high-dose capsules in people who have never had heart disease.

The trials people mix up

REDUCE-IT tested 4 grams a day of icosapent ethyl, a purified EPA prescription product, in people with high triglycerides who were already on a statin (PMID 30415628). Cardiovascular events were lower in that selected group. STRENGTH tested a different high-dose omega-3 carboxylic acid against corn oil and did not show a benefit (PMID 33190147). Those two results do not average out to "take 4 grams of the grocery-store bottle."

A 2025 review of omega-3 and atrial fibrillation describes a higher rhythm risk as the dose goes up (PMID 39617283). Chest symptoms, a racing heart, or a known rhythm problem are reasons to talk to a clinician before a high dose. Blood-thinning drugs are another.

A practical label check

Add EPA and DHA. Something in the few-hundred-milligram range matches the general-health target. Jamieson Omega 3 Select, Health Canada NPN 80002596, lists fish oil 1,000 mg providing 300 mg EPA and 200 mg DHA. That is 500 mg of the two acids, and the NPN is a licence to sell in Canada, not a purity seal. Prescription-strength EPA is a different product and a different decision.
