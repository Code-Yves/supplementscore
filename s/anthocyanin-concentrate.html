Anthocyanin concentrate (bilberry / blackcurrant)

📅 Last reviewed: May 23, 2026 · Editorial team
Vascular · Vision · Polyphenol
Tier 3 — Trending

What it is

Concentrated water-soluble pigments (delphinidin, cyanidin, pelargonidin glycosides) from dark berries. Standardised bilberry extract (typically 25% anthocyanins) has moderate evidence for capillary fragility and microcirculation, with smaller-scale evidence for night-vision recovery and metabolic endpoints. Bioavailability is low overall but tissue concentrations in eye and vascular endothelium are surprisingly high relative to plasma.

Dose

160–320 mg standardised bilberry extract daily, or equivalent blackcurrant

Time of day & tips
