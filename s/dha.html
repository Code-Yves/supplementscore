DHA (standalone, algal)

Brain · Pregnancy · Vegan
Tier 2 — Promising

DHA (standalone, algal) is a tier 2 (promising evidence) supplement with a SupplementScore composite rating of 75/100. The primary structural omega-3 fat in the brain and retina, sourced from algae instead of fish. Typical dose: 200–1,000 mg/day algal DHA.

What it is

The primary structural omega-3 fat in the brain and retina, sourced from algae instead of fish. Provides the same DHA found in fish oil but is suitable for vegans and vegetarians. Especially important during pregnancy for fetal brain development. Algal DHA is sustainably produced and free from ocean pollutants like mercury and PCBs. Very safe at recommended doses.
