Calcium

Bone · Muscle · Nerve
Tier 1 — Strong Evidence

Calcium is a tier 1 (strong evidence) supplement with a SupplementScore composite rating of 80/100. Critical for bone density, muscle contraction, and nerve signaling. Typical dose: 500–1,000 mg/day supplemental in divided doses.

What it is

Critical for bone density, muscle contraction, and nerve signaling. Most beneficial for postmenopausal women and those with low dietary intake. Maximum 500 mg per dose — split if taking more. Some meta-analyses link high supplement doses (1,000+ mg/day) to modest cardiovascular risk; food sources are preferable. Citrate form absorbs better than carbonate with low stomach acid.

Time of day & tips

Take with meals for better absorption. Never take more than 500 mg at once. Avoid taking with iron, zinc, or magnesium at the same time — they compete for absorption.

Is Calcium safe to take long-term?

Calcium has a safety rating of 4/5 in the SupplementScore methodology. Standard doses are well-tolerated in most adults. As with any supplement, consult a clinician if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, on prescription medication, or managing a chronic condition.
