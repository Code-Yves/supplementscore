Korean red ginseng (Panax ginseng)

📅 Last reviewed: May 23, 2026 · Editorial team
Adaptogen · Energy · Sexual function
Tier 2 — Promising

Korean red ginseng (fermented) is a tier 3 (emerging evidence) supplement with a SupplementScore composite rating of 58/100. Heat-processed and fermented form of Panax ginseng that converts ginsenosides into more bioavailable forms. Typical dose: 1–3 g/day fermented Korean red ginseng.

What it is

Steam-treated Panax ginseng root, which converts ginsenosides (Rb1, Rg1) into rarer compounds (Rg3, Rh2) with distinct pharmacology from white ginseng. Meta-analyses support modest improvements in erectile function, fatigue, immune function, and glycaemic control. Evidence for cognition is mixed but positive in older adults. More stimulating than American ginseng — prefer American if anxiety-prone. Drug interactions are meaningful: avoid with warfarin, MAOIs, and certain diabetes medications.

Frequently asked about Korean red ginseng (fermented)

What is Korean red ginseng (fermented)?

Heat-processed and fermented form of Panax ginseng that converts ginsenosides into more bioavailable forms. Multiple Korean trials show significant improvement in erectile function and libido in men.

How much Korean red ginseng (fermented) should I take?

1–3 g/day fermented Korean red ginseng; avoid with warfarin and stimulants

When should I take Korean red ginseng (fermented) and what should I know?

Take in the morning with water to avoid sleep disruption. Avoid with blood thinners and caffeine. Take with food if it causes nausea.

What is the evidence rating for Korean red ginseng (fermented)?

Korean red ginseng (fermented) has a SupplementScore composite rating of 58/100 (Tier 3 — emerging evidence). The rating combines efficacy, safety, research depth, onset, cost, and drug-interaction profile.
