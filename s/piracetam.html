What it is

The original racetam nootropic, synthesised in 1964 by Corneliu Giurgea who coined the term "nootropic." Modulates AMPA and NMDA receptors and improves membrane fluidity. Cochrane review found insufficient evidence for cognitive enhancement in healthy adults but modest benefits in age-related cognitive decline. Approved as a prescription drug in many European countries. Very safe profile.

Is Piracetam safe to take long-term?

Piracetam has a safety rating of 4/5 in the SupplementScore methodology. Standard doses are well-tolerated in most adults. As with any supplement, consult a clinician if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, on prescription medication, or managing a chronic condition.
