What it is

A traditional supplement that provides omega-3 EPA and DHA alongside preformed vitamins A and D. Good option for people in northern climates with limited sun exposure. However, the vitamin A content limits how much you can safely take — high doses risk vitamin A toxicity, especially in pregnancy. Modern concentrated fish oil provides more EPA and DHA per capsule without the vitamin A ceiling.
