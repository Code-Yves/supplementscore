Homepage hero options

Preview only. These are not on the homepage. Titles, descriptions, dates, and read times are the articles in today’s carousel. Previous and Next, the arrow keys, and a sideways swipe move through those 20 articles. Variations of options 2 and 6 follow those two layouts. No category pills.

1. Minimal text feature

One article, type only, on the same teal field. Stays off phones, like the carousel.

Hidden on phones.

2. Split figure and text

A figure from the article on the left, the story on the right. These articles have no photographs, so the number is the picture. Desktop only.

Hidden on phones.

Variations of option 2

2a. Wide figure, light headline

The teal figure takes more than half the card. The headline is a lighter weight. Desktop only.

Hidden on phones.

2b. Cream number, teal story

A narrow cream column holds only the figure. The story sits on teal, with arrow controls. Desktop only.

Hidden on phones.

2c. Figure on the right

The story is on the left in a lighter headline, the teal figure on the right. Earlier and Later sit above the title. On a phone the figure stacks on top.

2d. Equal split, number only

Each half is the same width. The figure is the number alone. Previous and Next sit on a bar under both columns. Desktop only.

Hidden on phones.

2e. Figure as a band

The figure runs across the top as a teal band, and the story sits underneath in a lighter headline. This would show on phones.

3. Three latest articles

A short list of the three newest pieces in the carousel, by date. This would show on phones.

4. Large card with position dots

One story on a cream card. Twenty dots mark the place in the same rotation. This would show on phones.

5. Side-scrolling strip

A horizontal row of the first articles in the rotation. The next card stays in view. This would show on phones.

6. Magazine grid

One lead story and two shorter ones beside it. On a phone the two shorter stories stack under the lead.

Variations of option 6

6a. Figure in the lead

Option 2’s teal figure sits inside the lead story, with two titles beside it. On a phone the figure, the lead, and the titles stack.

6b. Teal column for the other stories

A wide, light headline on cream. The next two stories are titles on a teal column, and the arrows live in that column.

6c. Figure beside the headline

A teal lead with the figure beside a lighter headline. Two titles are listed underneath, without descriptions.

6d. Even columns, a line from each

Lead and secondary stories share the width. Each secondary story keeps one line from the article. Earlier and Later run along the bottom.

6e. Split figure as the lead

The lead is option 2’s split, arrows inside the figure. Two titles sit underneath in equal columns.

7. Numbered index

The rotation as a numbered list, with the selected article written out beside it. Desktop only.

Hidden on phones.

8. Open masthead

No card. The story sits on the page in the same type as the homepage headline. This would show on phones.

9. Two equal stories

Two articles share the row, same size, split by a rule. Desktop only.

Hidden on phones.

10. Lead line and what follows

A short teal band for the current story, then the next three titles underneath. This would show on phones, stacked.
