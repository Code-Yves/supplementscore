Ten ways to replace the article carousel

Preview only. These are not on the homepage. Titles, descriptions, dates, and read times are the articles in today’s carousel. Previous and Next, the arrow keys, and a sideways swipe move through those 20 articles. No category pills.

1. Minimal text feature

One article, type only, on the same teal field. Stays off phones, like the carousel.

Hidden on phones.

2. Split figure and text

A figure from the article on the left, the story on the right. These articles have no photographs, so the number is the picture. Desktop only.

Hidden on phones.

3. Three latest articles

A short list of the three newest pieces in the carousel, by date. This would show on phones.

4. Large card with position dots

One story on a cream card. Twenty dots mark the place in the same rotation. This would show on phones.

5. Side-scrolling strip

A horizontal row of the first articles in the rotation. The next card stays in view. This would show on phones.

6. Magazine grid

One lead story and two shorter ones beside it. On a phone the two shorter stories stack under the lead.

7. Numbered index

The rotation as a numbered list, with the selected article written out beside it. Desktop only.

Hidden on phones.

8. Open masthead

No card. The story sits on the page in the same type as the homepage headline. This would show on phones.

9. Two equal stories

Two articles share the row, same size, split by a rule. Desktop only.

Hidden on phones.

10. Lead line and what follows

A short teal band for the current story, then the next three titles underneath. This would show on phones, stacked.
